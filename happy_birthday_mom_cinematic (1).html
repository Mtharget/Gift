✦A message from the heart✦

From the very first breath I took,
you were there — my shelter, my sunrise, my home.

Your hands have built more than you know —
a life, a legacy, a love that echoes in everything I am.

You taught me that strength is gentle,
that grace is powerful,
and that the most beautiful thing in the world
is a mother’s unconditional love.

Today, the universe celebrates the day
it gave us you — and we are forever grateful.

May this birthday bring you joy
as boundless as the love you give.
